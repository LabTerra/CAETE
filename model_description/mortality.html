Mortality

In the current version of CAETÊ, mortality is not mechanistically represented; PLS are eliminated through environmental filtering, which includes climatic conditions and trade-off relations between the variant functional traits and the trait parameters, leading to carbon starvation when a PLS fails to maintain a minimum biomass threshold.

Once eliminated, there is no possibility of reestablishment.
